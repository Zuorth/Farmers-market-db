# Farmers-market-db
Farmers Market Vendor Management System

A relational database project for CIS 344 (Fall 2026) that models how a regional farmers market organization tracks its markets, market days, vendors, products, and sales.

What this project does

The database tracks:

Markets and the specific market days they operate on
Vendors who apply to sell at those markets, including their permit info and product category
Products each vendor sells
Attendance, which vendors showed up on which market day, and what stall fee they paid
Sales, individual transactions tying a product to a market day
